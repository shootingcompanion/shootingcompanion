Introduction

In target shooting competitions it's common to plot your shots for record keeping. Plot-o-matic style plotting is becoming more and more popular as it is able to visually represent what the wind is doing to your shots during the course of fire and enable competitors make better judgements on the wind conditions.

This app allows you to seamlessly switch between traditional plots and Plot-o-matic. Shots can be entered as simply as tapping your aim point, tapping the point of impact, then tapping save! Adjust the scope settings in the app as you adjust your turrets and you'll always be in sync with your plot.

It works with MOA scopes (1/4 and 1/8 MOA clicks) and mil scopes (0.1 and 0.05 mil clicks), and comes with many US, UK and international targets - and if yours isn't there, you can make your own.

It runs on iPhone and iPad, and your sessions sync between them through your own iCloud account, so you can plot on your phone at the range and look back over it on your iPad at home.

Session details are exportable to CSV (for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets), with scores, scope settings, the target, and aim and impact points as MOA or mils from centre - so you can run your own analysis on your own data.

This app has been developed by an F-Class shooter in his spare time, as other apps on the market were lacking something. Every purchase goes straight to funding the hobby!

For useful references on Plot-o-matic plotting you can either read 'The Wind Book for Rifle Shooters' By Linda K. Miller and Keith A. Cunningham or refer to the excellent article 'How to improve your score through strategy' by Adam MacDonald (autotrickler.com). Please note the developer is not affiliated with any of these authors, but can highly recommend both.
